About

I'm a Principal Software Engineer at Dell Technologies with 14 years of building backend and cloud platforms. I work on distributed systems and Apache Kafka. Outside of work I spend most of my time on search and observability with OpenSearch.

This blog is where I learn in public. Every post comes from something I built and tested in a lab: a pipeline I wired up, a bug I tracked down, or a feature I wanted to understand from the inside. I write them so you can follow along on your own machine.
